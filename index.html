Areas of Professional Practice

Services rendered in accordance with the regulatory framework of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI).

01

Statutory Audit & Assurance

Execution of independent audits pursuant to the Companies Act, 2013 and the Income Tax Act, 1961. Formulation of audit reports ensuring compliance with Indian Accounting Standards (Ind AS) and ICAI auditing standards.

02

Direct & Indirect Taxation

Preparation, assessment, and filing of corporate, firm, and individual tax returns. Representation before appellate authorities and factual advisory on tax compliance frameworks under the Income Tax Act and GST regulations.

03

Corporate Secretarial Compliance

Statutory filings with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA). Procedural compliance for company incorporation, maintenance of statutory registers, board resolutions, and routine secretarial obligations.
