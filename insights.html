Firm Legacy

75 Years of Professional Excellence

M/s C S HARIHARAN & CO LLP has rich exposure in diverse sectors across a wider spectrum of audit and consultancy assignments. As the firm enters its 75th year, it is bestowed with the advantage of the best of both worlds: a knowledgeable young team guided by professional excellence and the wisdom of our senior team.

Originally started in 1949 by Shri. C.S. HARIHARAN as a sole proprietary concern, it officially became a firm of Chartered Accountants in Practice on 01-04-1956. Shri. C.S. Hariharan was a GDA and was registered as an RA with the Ministry of Commerce, Government of India. Subsequently, with the formation of the ICAI in 1949, he was absorbed as an FCA (Membership No. 1196).

He was later joined by his two sons, Mr. C.H. Subramanian and Mr. C.H. Ramakrishnan, who served as partners of the firm for more than 50 years and were the guiding force of the practice till their very end. Today, Mr. C.S. Hariharan (grandson of Mr. Hariharan Senior) represents the third-generation Chartered Accountant successfully running the firm.

To remain in tune with the times, the firm was converted into an LLP in 2021. It is currently looked after by Managing Partner Mr. V. Saravanan FCA, supported by Mr. S. Seetharaman FCA, and Mr. C.S. Hariharan, who is presently engaged in mentoring start-ups and raising critical funding for them, including within the Fintech sector.
