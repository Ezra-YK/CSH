Our History

A Legacy of Trust (1949–Present)

Entering its 75th year of practice, M/s C.S. Hariharan & Co LLP provides comprehensive audit and consultancy services across a diverse spectrum of industries. Our practice is built on a rare convergence of strengths: the rigorous, modern expertise of a young professional team anchored by the enduring wisdom and operational excellence of our senior leadership.

The firm’s origins date back to 1949 when it was established as a sole proprietorship by Shri. C.S. Hariharan. Originally a Government Diploma in Accountancy (GDA) holder and a Registered Accountant (RA) with the Ministry of Commerce, he became one of the early members of the ICAI, absorbed as a Fellow Chartered Accountant (Membership No. 1196). The practice was officially established as a firm of Chartered Accountants on April 1, 1956.

His sons, Mr. C.H. Subramanian and Mr. C.H. Ramakrishnan, later joined the practice and served as partners for over five decades. They acted as the firm's guiding force until their passing, cementing a reputation for integrity that defines the firm today. That legacy is now carried forward by the founder's grandson, Mr. C.S. Hariharan, representing the third generation of Chartered Accountants at the helm.
